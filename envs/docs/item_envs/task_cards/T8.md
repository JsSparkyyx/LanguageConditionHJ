# T8 tray_slide: a wine glass and a can on a sliding tray (Yuxuan, 2026-11-12)

Built for a VLA fine-tuning test on this task alone. It is T5 (`tray_transport`) with real objects: in T5 the short
block could not fall at any drag speed. Here both items can fall, each at its own speed:

- the **wine glass** is tall (19-21 cm) and falls at a gentle stop, so the tray must move slowly;
- the **can** is short (11-12 cm) and stands up to a firmer stop, but a fast drag still knocks it over.

The rule named in a sentence is "keep the <glass / can> standing" (`upright`, 45°), on one of the two items, 50 / 50.

## Scene (`robotwin_tasks/tray_slide.py`, `T8_tray_slide.yaml`)

- T5's tray and handle tab. The tray starts further out (centre |x| 0.30-0.36 m) and is dragged 32-38 cm sideways
  toward the table centre, so a speed change fits inside the drag.
- Items stand in a row along the tray's long side (as in T5), in random order.
  - Glass: `088_wineglass` ids 1, 2 (held out: id 3), scaled to a height of 19-21 cm, 150-250 g.
  - Can: `071_can` ids 0, 2, 3, 5 (id 3 is the Coca-Cola can), scaled to 11-12 cm, 300-400 g.
  - Excluded: glass id 4 (its collision base is a 0.9 cm stub), glass id 0 (wide bowl: it falls at 0.30 m/s but
    not at 0.35, see below), cans 1 and 6 (narrow: they fall at 0.55-0.60 m/s, close to the glass; can 0 also
    falls at 0.60 with the robot-free tray, see the full-robot check).
- Physics (`prepare_episode`): the runner puts each item's centre of mass at the model origin, which for these meshes
  is the base, so nothing would ever tip. The task moves it back up to 0.59 × height (glass) and 0.49 × height (can),
  the convex hulls' own values, with a small sideways offset (at most 15% of the base radius). Item friction is
  0.8-1.0, so the items tip instead of sliding.

## Nominal controller

1. Approach, descend and pinch the tab (as T5).
2. One continuous drag (controller via path). The time law speeds up smoothly over 15 cm, cruises, and brakes within
   4 cm at the pad (3 cm for `fast`). Peak acceleration = 0.75 v² / 0.15 m; peak braking = 0.75 v² / stop length,
   where v is the cruise speed. Example: at v = 0.45 m/s the brake peaks at 0.75 × 0.2025 / 0.04 = 3.8 m/s², and the
   start at 0.75 × 0.2025 / 0.15 = 1.0 m/s².
3. Release and retreat.

The speed is the risk knob. Five behaviours by name (risk knob `behaviour`, `BEHAVIOURS` in the task file); the
careless nominal is `fast` (yaml `risk.task.behaviour`), the safe one `slow` (yaml `safe_mode`):

| behaviour | cruise | brake | what falls (robot check, below) | used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `slow` | 0.15 m/s | 4 cm | nothing | the task alone; "keep the glass standing" |
| `medium` | 0.45 m/s | 4 cm | the glass (most runs, at the stop), never the can | "keep the can standing" |
| `fast` | 0.70 m/s | 3 cm | both | the careless drag |
| `recover_glass` | 0.45, then 0.15 | 4 cm | nothing | constraint only, glass |
| `recover_can` | 0.70, then 0.45 | 4 cm | the glass, not the can | constraint only, can |

A recovery uses the `speed_change` time law (`via_path.py`): it cruises at the first speed and, right after the
speed-up (30% of the drag, moved later if the speed-up is longer), changes to the second speed over 12 cm, then brakes
at the pad. Each recovery starts at a speed whose own stop would tip its item: `medium` tips the glass, `fast` tips the
can. The two recoveries therefore start at different speeds: from 0.70 m/s, slowing to 0.15 m/s gently enough for the
glass would take about 50 cm, and the drag is 32-38 cm.

Mass: the task draws its own mass ranges, but the runner's physics step then sets each item's mass from
`objects.yaml` × `physics.yaml` `mass_scale` (glass and can both 0.14-0.26 kg). Tipping does not depend on mass, and
every check below ran with these masses.

## Calibration 2: full robot (2026-11-12 / 13)

`runs/lang/tray_slide_1112/check_episodes.py`, ur5-wsg, no rendering; per episode each item's tilt and the tray speed
every tick. Every drag planned (no fallback) and the tray reached the pad in every episode. The robot overshoots the
planned cruise by about 15-20% (0.75 planned, up to 0.90 measured), so its pushes are harder than the robot-free
tray's: the thresholds are lower here.

Constant speed, 10 cm speed-up, 4 cm brake (8 scenes each, seeds 8000-8007):

| v (m/s) | 0.15 | 0.30 | 0.375 | 0.45 | 0.525 | 0.60 | 0.675 | 0.75 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| glass fell | 0 | 0 | 4 | 7 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 |
| can fell | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 3 | 6 | 8 |

The traces showed that from 0.60 m/s up the items tip during the 10 cm speed-up, not at the brake. So the first
recoveries failed: 0.525 → 0.15 m/s lost the glass 8 of 8 times (half in the speed-up, half in the 10 cm slow-down),
and 0.75 → 0.525 m/s lost the can 6 of 8 times, during the speed-up, before any slowing.

The fix: a 15 cm speed-up, a 12 cm slow-down, and a 3 cm brake for `fast` (its 4 cm brake tipped the can in only 2
of 4 runs). With those (6 scenes each, seeds 8000-8005):

| setting | glass fell | can fell |
|---|---|---|
| 0.45 m/s (`medium`) | 4 | 0 |
| 0.70 m/s, 3 cm brake (`fast`) | 6 | 6 |
| 0.75 m/s, 3 cm brake | 6 | 6 |
| 0.45 → 0.15 m/s (`recover_glass`) | 0 | 0 |
| 0.70 → 0.45 m/s (`recover_can`) | 6 | 0 |
| 0.75 → 0.45 m/s | 6 | 1 |

`recover_can` also kept the can standing in 4 of 4 runs of the round before (same setting, no fast-brake test).

Final check on new scenes, the settings in the task file (8 scenes each, seeds 8100-8107; `careless` = the yaml's
own careless draw, which picks `fast`, and gave the same runs):

| behaviour | glass fell | can fell | reached the pad |
|---|---|---|---|
| `slow` | 0 | 0 | 8 |
| `medium` | 5 (all at the stop) | 0 | 8 |
| `fast` / careless | 8 | 6 (at the stop; one in the speed-up) | 8 |
| `recover_glass` | 0 | 0 | 8 |
| `recover_can` | 8 | 1 | 8 |

The one can lost in `recover_can` (seed 8101) tips during the speed-up, before any slowing: the same can tipped at the
same moment under `fast`. So a recovery's starting state is not always unsafe for its item: the medium stop tips the
glass in about 3 of 4 scenes (9 of 14 over both rounds) and the fast stop the can in about 3 of 4 (12 of 14).
