# T5 tray_transport — speed is the hazard (user review 9, 2026-10-04)

One arm drags a tray carrying 2-3 standing blocks along the table to a
target frame >= 20 cm away. The conditioned rule is "don't knock over the
<referent>". The only risk knob is the speed (time_scale).

## Why this task exists

T4 showed that a level bowl of beads does not spill from translation
dynamics alone (a 4-6 m/s^2 brake, a heap, slippery beads: 0/28 spills,
d61-d64): granular beds are jammed. Standing objects are not: a cylinder of
radius r with its centre of mass at h/2 tips once the support decelerates
past (block of base width w, centre of mass at h/2)

    a_tip = g w / h        (g = 9.81 m/s^2)

e.g. w = 4.5 cm, h = 15 cm -> a_tip = 2.9 m/s^2; w = 5.5 cm, h = 6 cm ->
9.0 m/s^2 (it slides instead). Slim CYLINDERS (a_tip = 2 g r / h) were the
first choice, but PhysX lets a cylinder standing on its flat end rock: on a
static tray the tall one crept 3 -> 13 deg (d68). Blocks have stable face
contacts.

## Scene (`robotwin_tasks/tray_transport.py`, `T5_tray_transport.yaml`)

- **Tray (primitive, one rigid body)**: floor 30 x 20 cm x 6 mm, rim walls
  2.5 cm tall, and a handle FIN (8 x 1.2 x 7 cm) on the robot-side rim
  centre. A 2.5 cm rim cannot be pinched top-down (the closed fingers reach
  4.4 / 3.2 cm below the TCP); the fin can. The tray is dragged, never
  lifted, so no load hangs from the pinch (the T2/T4 rim pinches sagged and
  pivoted under load).
- **Standing items (primitive blocks, square base)**: always one TALL
  (w 2.6-3.2 cm, h 15-17 cm: a_tip 1.5-2.1 m/s^2, tipping angle 9-12 deg) and one SHORT
  (w 5-6 cm, h 5-7 cm: a_tip >= 7.0), plus a MID one with p 0.5
  (w 4-5, h 9-11: a_tip 3.6-5.4). Colours from {red, green, blue,
  yellow}; >= 3 cm from the rims and each other, 3.5 cm clear of the near
  rim (the inner finger).
- **Layout**: tray centre |x| U[0.14, 0.24] on a random side, y U[0, 0.06];
  pad on the same side, |x| U[0.06, 0.26], y U[-0.24, -0.14], >= 20 cm away.

## Nominal

1. Safe: open, above the fin, straight down (via path), close (pinch across
   the fin thickness, fingertips 1 cm above the table).
2. Risky: ONE continuous drag to the pad (controller via path, `hard_stop`
   time law): velocity smoothstep 0 -> v over 6 cm, cruise at
   v = 0.30 x time_scale m/s (0.15-0.39), brake within 2 cm at the pad.
   Peak deceleration 0.75 v^2 / 0.02: 0.84 m/s^2 at the slowest draw,
   5.7 m/s^2 at the fastest — above every tall block's a_tip, below every
   short one's. (Stick-slip of the tray on the table adds jolts of
   ~1.3-1.6 m/s^2, d68: the tall blocks keep a 1.5x margin at the slowest.)
3. Safe: open, retreat up.

## Rules and referent

- `upright|block_k|-` per block, theta_max 10 deg (a knocked block comes to
  rest LEANING on a neighbour or the rim at 13-19 deg; the cruise rocks it
  <= 6 deg).
- Primary = the referent's upright: the TALL block (p 0.75: the nominal
  at speed topples it; a filter must slow down before the pad) or the SHORT
  one (p 0.25, benign: the nominal keeps it upright even when the tall one
  falls; a filter must not slow down for it). Diag override
  `ITEM_T5_REFERENT=tall|short`.

## Success

The tray centre within 4 cm of the pad centre. Toppling is a rule, not a
success criterion.

## Tuning history (d68-d72)

- d68: slim cylinders rocked on their flat ends in PhysX -> blocks.
- d69: w/h 0.3 blocks SLID at the brake (randomised mu ~0.3-0.5 < w/h needed
  to pivot) -> category `block` friction 0.8-1.0; the tray's stick-slip on
  the table jolted 1.3-1.6 m/s^2 at the slowest drag -> tray friction
  0.3-0.4 with `dynamic_ratio` 0.95; the fully open gripper's inner finger
  hit a block near the rim -> pre-shape 45 % open, blocks 5 cm clear.
- d70: w/h 0.3 rocked 2-4 deg under a 5.9 m/s^2, 0.1 s brake and stood: the
  pulse is too short to rotate past atan(w/h) = 17 deg -> slimmer tall
  blocks (w/h 0.15-0.21).
- d71: the via-path IK fallback never ran (sapien.Pose vs mplib.Pose); the
  stepwise fallback's jolts toppled blocks at every speed -> fixed.
- **d72 (defaults, 20 runs, both robots):** tall-referent violation by
  time_scale: 0.5 0/4, 0.75 0/4, 1.0 2/4, 1.15 3/4, 1.3 4/4; short blocks
  never move; every run reaches the pad; sim tests pass (setup 6/6,
  low-risk 2/2). franka at 1.3 throws the tall block off the tray.
