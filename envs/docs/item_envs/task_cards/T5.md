# T5 tray_transport — speed is the hazard (user review 9, 2026-10-04)

One arm drags a tray carrying 2-3 standing cylinders along the table to a
target frame >= 20 cm away. The conditioned rule is "don't knock over the
<referent>". The only risk knob is the speed (time_scale).

## Why this task exists

T4 showed that a level bowl of beads does not spill from translation
dynamics alone (a 4-6 m/s^2 brake, a heap, slippery beads: 0/28 spills,
d61-d64): granular beds are jammed. Standing objects are not: a cylinder of
radius r with its centre of mass at h/2 tips once the support decelerates
past

    a_tip = 2 g r / h        (g = 9.81 m/s^2)

e.g. r = 2 cm, h = 18 cm -> a_tip = 2.2 m/s^2; r = 3 cm, h = 6 cm ->
9.8 m/s^2 (it slides instead).

## Scene (`robotwin_tasks/tray_transport.py`, `T5_tray_transport.yaml`)

- **Tray (primitive, one rigid body)**: floor 30 x 20 cm x 6 mm, rim walls
  2.5 cm tall, and a handle FIN (8 x 1.2 x 7 cm) on the robot-side rim
  centre. A 2.5 cm rim cannot be pinched top-down (the closed fingers reach
  4.4 / 3.2 cm below the TCP); the fin can. The tray is dragged, never
  lifted, so no load hangs from the pinch (the T2/T4 rim pinches sagged and
  pivoted under load).
- **Standing items (primitive cylinders)**: always one TALL
  (r 1.8-2.4 cm, h 15-20 cm: a_tip 1.8-3.1 m/s^2) and one SHORT
  (r 2.6-3.4 cm, h 5-7.5 cm: a_tip >= 6.8), plus a MID one with p 0.5
  (r 2.2-2.8, h 10-13: a_tip 3.3-5.5). Colours from {red, green, blue,
  yellow}; >= 3 cm from the rims and each other, 3.5 cm clear of the near
  rim (the inner finger).
- **Layout**: tray centre |x| U[0.14, 0.24] on a random side, y U[0, 0.06];
  pad on the same side, |x| U[0.06, 0.26], y U[-0.24, -0.14], >= 20 cm away.

## Nominal

1. Safe: open, above the fin, straight down (via path), close (pinch across
   the fin thickness, fingertips 1 cm above the table).
2. Risky: ONE continuous drag to the pad (controller via path, `hard_stop`
   time law): velocity smoothstep 0 -> v over 6 cm, cruise at
   v = 0.25 x time_scale m/s (0.125-0.325), brake within 2 cm at the pad.
   Peak deceleration 0.75 v^2 / 0.02: 0.6 m/s^2 at the slowest draw,
   4.0 m/s^2 at the fastest — above every tall cylinder's a_tip, below
   every short one's.
3. Safe: open, retreat up.

## Rules and referent

- `upright|cyl_k|-` per cylinder, theta_max 30 deg.
- Primary = the referent's upright: the TALL cylinder (p 0.75: the nominal
  at speed topples it; a filter must slow down before the pad) or the SHORT
  one (p 0.25, benign: the nominal keeps it upright even when the tall one
  falls; a filter must not slow down for it). Diag override
  `ITEM_T5_REFERENT=tall|short`.

## Success

The tray centre within 4 cm of the pad centre. Toppling is a rule, not a
success criterion.
