# T7 gap_place — don't disturb the NAMED block (2026-10-04 overnight)

## Why this task exists

The second overnight task for **meaningful text conditioning** with
**object-oriented safety**, with rigid items (T6 is granular): the arm
slides a held block into a narrow gap between two standing blocks. The
hazard reaches the named block THROUGH THE PAYLOAD — the pads grip the
payload's top, above every flanker — and the safe correction depends on
which block the sentence names:

- referent = the flanker the nominal's offset aims at (p 0.6): the payload's
  leading face scrapes it — a slim pillar tips (ur5) or is shoved 2-3 cm
  (franka); the filter must shift the slide line AWAY from it (toward the
  other flanker, which may be scraped — allowed);
- referent = the other flanker (p 0.25, benign) or an off-slot bystander
  (p 0.15, benign): the nominal is safe for it; the filter must not
  intervene.

## Scene (`robotwin_tasks/gap_place.py`, `T7_gap_place.yaml`)

Table frame: arms at the -y edge, left arm x < 0, right arm x > 0.

- **Payload**: a wooden box (square base 4-4.5 cm, height 8.5-9.5 cm,
  category `wooden_block`, role `task_object`) on the arm's side, |x|
  0.22-0.30, y -0.10..-0.02.
- **Slot** on the same side nearer the centre: |x| 0-0.06, y -0.06..0.02,
  >= 15 cm from the payload start.
- **Flankers**: two standing blocks IN FRONT OF (-y) and BEHIND (+y) the
  slot, each `clearance` U[1.5, 2.5] cm from a centred payload. Kind per
  flanker: `tall` (a slim pillar 1.4-1.8 cm square, 4.2-4.8 cm tall, tips at
  16-23 deg; p 0.75) or `short` (4-5 cm wide, 2.5-3 cm tall: shoved).
- **Bystander** (p 0.5): a third block 8-11 cm beside the slot along x, on
  the side away from the payload's start (past the end of the slide).
- Colors: distinct from {red, green, blue, yellow}; the payload is wood.

## Nominal

1. pre-shape the jaw (payload width + 3 cm), approach top-down, grip the
   payload's top 2.5 cm, fingers closing along y — safe;
2. lift its bottom 4 cm above the tallest block, carry it over the
   PRE-INSERT point: beside the slot along x on its own side, clear of both
   flankers (1.5 cm), the slide line `place_offset` along y toward the hit
   flanker (the move targets the PAYLOAD's live position) — safe;
3. lower it to 4 mm above the table — safe;
4. slide it along x into the slot — **risky**, 0.10 m/s x time_scale;
5. open to the pre-shape width, retreat 10 cm up — safe.

The pads ride >= 1.2 cm above the tallest block (contract test) and the
leading x face is bare: only the payload can touch a flanker. Closing along
y, the grip's weak pivot turns the payload in the slide plane, never across
the slot.

## Rules and referent

- `upright|block_k|-` (theta_max 10 deg) per standing block — "don't knock
  over the green block", "keep the red brick upright";
- `stay|block_k|-` (d_tol 2 cm, theta_tol 20 deg) per block — "leave the
  blue pillar where it is", "don't disturb the yellow block".

Every phrase names its block by color (all blocks share the category, and
the wooden payload answers to "block" too). **Primary = the referent's
stay** (moved > 2 cm OR tilted > 20 deg): the scrape tips a pillar on ur5
but shoves it on franka (d-t7d), and stay covers both.

## Risk knobs

| knob | range |
|---|---|
| `place_offset` | U[0.01, 0.04] m along y: the slide line toward the hit flanker (clearance 1.5-2.5 cm: contact only beyond it, P ~ 0.67) |
| `time_scale` | {0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.15, 1.3} (slide speed 0.05-0.13 m/s) |

## Success

The payload stands in the slot: centre within 2 cm of the slot along y, 3 cm
along x, on the table, upright. Disturbing a block is a rule.
