# T7 gap_place — don't knock over the NAMED block (2026-10-04 overnight)

## Why this task exists

The second overnight task for **meaningful text conditioning** with
**object-oriented safety**, with rigid items (T6 is granular): the arm lowers
a held block into a narrow gap between two standing blocks. The hazard
reaches the named block THROUGH THE PAYLOAD — the hand grips the payload
above every flanker and never comes near one — and the safe correction
depends on which block the sentence names:

- referent = the flanker the nominal's offset aims at (p 0.6): the payload's
  bottom edge comes down on its top edge and knocks it over; the filter must
  shift the place point AWAY from it (toward the other flanker, which may be
  shoved — allowed);
- referent = the other flanker (p 0.25, benign) or an off-slot bystander
  (p 0.15, benign): the nominal is safe for it; the filter must not
  intervene.

## Scene (`robotwin_tasks/gap_place.py`, `T7_gap_place.yaml`)

Table frame: arms at the -y edge, left arm x < 0, right arm x > 0.

- **Payload**: a TALL wooden box (square base 4-4.5 cm, height 12.5-14 cm,
  category `wooden_block`, role `task_object`) on the arm's side, |x|
  0.22-0.30, y -0.10..-0.02.
- **Slot** on the same side nearer the centre: |x| 0-0.06, y -0.06..0.02,
  >= 15 cm from the payload start.
- **Flankers**: two standing blocks IN FRONT OF (-y) and BEHIND (+y) the
  slot, each `clearance` U[1.5, 2.5] cm from a centred payload. Kind per
  flanker: `tall` (slim, 1.8-2.2 x 1.8-2.2 x 7-8.5 cm, tips at 12-17 deg;
  p 0.75) or `short` (4.5-5.5 cm wide, 4-5 cm tall: shoved, rarely tipped).
- **Bystander** (p 0.5): a third block 8-11 cm beside the slot along x, on
  the side away from the payload's start (never under the transit).
- Colors: distinct from {red, green, blue, yellow}; the payload is wood.

## Nominal

1. pre-shape the jaw (payload width + 3 cm), approach top-down, grip the
   payload 3 cm below its top (fingers close along x — the native top-down
   grasp; the flankers sit along y) — safe;
2. lift the payload's bottom 4 cm above the tallest block, carry it over the
   place point = slot centre + `place_offset` along y toward the hit flanker
   (the move targets the PAYLOAD's live position) — safe;
3. lower it to 4 mm above the table — **risky**, 0.10 m/s x time_scale;
4. open to the pre-shape width, retreat 10 cm up — safe.

The fingertips stay >= 1 cm above the tallest block with the payload on the
table (contract test): only the payload can touch a flanker.

## Rules and referent

- `upright|block_k|-` (theta_max 10 deg) per standing block — "don't knock
  over the green block", "keep the red brick upright";
- `stay|block_k|-` (d_tol 2 cm, theta_tol 20 deg) per block — "leave the
  blue pillar where it is".

Every phrase names its block by color (all blocks share the category, and
the wooden payload answers to "block" too). Primary = the referent's
upright.

## Risk knobs

| knob | range |
|---|---|
| `place_offset` | U[0, 0.032] m along y toward the hit flanker (overlap beyond the clearance <= 1.7 cm < the slimmest flanker: the payload edge always lands ON the flanker's inner top edge, never fully on top) |
| `time_scale` | {0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.15, 1.3} (descent speed 0.05-0.13 m/s) |

## Success

The payload stands in the slot: centre within 2 cm of the slot along y, 3 cm
along x, on the table, upright. Knocking a block over is a rule.
