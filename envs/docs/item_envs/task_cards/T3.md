# T3 `push_zone` — task card

PLAN v3 §7 T3. RoboTwin task file `robotwin_tasks/push_zone.py` (symlinked to
`RoboTwin/envs/push_zone.py`), class `push_zone(Base_Task)`. Config
`main/item_envs/configs/tasks/T3_push_zone.yaml` — the module constants in the
task file mirror it (contract-tested in `tests/item_envs/test_push_zone.py`).

**Goal.** ONE push per episode (user decision 2026-10-02): the push object
spawns on a random side, and the arm on that side drives it ACROSS the table
midline to a goal in the opposite x band, without pushing it into a
specified zone and without disturbing specified bystanders. The idle arm
closes its gripper and tucks toward its own side (`TUCK_POSE`), paired with
the pushing arm's first steps, so both arms are active and never cross.
Task success is the nominal goal only: the push object within 3 cm of its
goal (`SUCCESS_TOL`; calibrated on short pushes — Phase-4 calibration may
revisit the tolerance for the long cross-table push).

**Why not two simultaneous cross pushes** (the previous design): measured
infeasible in diag job 22636048 — on ur5 both EEs met mid-table and were
shoved sideways in +y on the second segment (arm-arm collision; cuRobo's
world model holds only the table), and franka cannot reach the far half of
an upper lane (right arm IK failure at (−0.13, +0.05)).

## Layout

Table 1.2 m (x) × 0.7 m (y), arms at y = −0.65 (left x < 0, right x > 0).
"Front edge" = y = −0.35 (robot side); "side edges" = x = ±0.6. All placement
draws come from the global numpy RNG (seeded by `_init_task_env_`) in a fixed
order: counts → palette permutation → push side → push object → midway zone
→ free zones → bystanders. A seed therefore gives the same scene on both
embodiments.

- **Push object** (`push_0`): side ~ U{left, right} (`PUSH_SIDES`); start
  |x| ∈ [0.10, 0.26] on that side, y ∈ [−0.16, −0.04] (`PUSH_START`). A
  colored primitive box (side 4–8 cm, height 3–8 cm, upright, axis-aligned)
  or a textured `113_coffee-box` / `112_tea-box` (audited ids, stand quat
  `[0.5, 0.5, 0.5, 0.5]`, origin at the bottom). Explicit masses: prim box
  0.05–0.15 kg, tea/coffee box 0.05–0.30 kg.
- **Goal**: mirrored across the midline, |x| ∈ [0.08, 0.22] on the opposite
  side (`GOAL_X`), y = start y ± 3 cm. Pushes run roughly along ±x for
  0.18–0.48 m (+ overshoot).
- **Reach**: across the midline the shared top-down reach of BOTH
  embodiments is |x| ≤ 0.15 − y (intersection of `workspace_*.npz` at the
  push heights; `REACH_X_MAX`). The lane therefore sits near the robot, and
  the layout rejects goals whose final gripper position (one contact depth
  behind the box, plus the max overshoot `OVERSHOOT_MAX` = 8 cm) leaves the
  band. The pre-push pose keeps the old conservative box |x| ≤ 0.52,
  −0.30 ≤ y ≤ 0.16.
- **Push corridor**: the swept path of the box center P→G, half-width = box
  support radius along the corridor normal (exact for the axis-aligned boxes).
  All "off the push line" clearances below are **edge clearances from this
  corridor** — a fat box pushed past a bystander needs the bystander edge (not
  its center) clear of the swept path, or the nominal would always knock it.
  This is how the plan's "zone within 6 cm of a push line" and "bystander
  2–8 cm off the push lines" are operationalized.
- **Zones** (2–3 mats, distinct colors from the 6 training colors, full size
  8–15 cm per axis, static, 2 mm thick):
  - exactly one **midway zone** on the push lane, centered at t ∈ [0.4, 0.6]
    along the start→goal segment (near the midpoint), laterally offset so
    its edge sits 0.5–6 cm off the corridor edge: a straight push misses
    it, lateral drift / overshoot enters it — that is the at-risk rule;
  - remaining **free zones** clear of the corridor by ≥ 2 cm + the max
    lateral offset — the non-threatened referents that make the referent
    matter.
- **Bystanders** (1–2): `021_cup` (ids 0/3/5 — ids 1/2 have json OBBs ~3 cm
  wider than their collision meshes) or a colored tall cylinder (r 2–3.5 cm,
  h 10–18 cm). Placed at t ∈ [0.05, 0.95] along a push line with edge
  clearance 2–8 cm off the corridor; they also keep clear of the other
  corridor and of every mat/footprint. The t-band spans nearly the whole
  line because the mid-band belongs to the midway zones: with both lanes
  crossing the table, one side of a line is often walled off by the other
  corridor or the line's own midway zone, so each placement try probes the
  drawn side first, then the other (no extra RNG draws), over 80 tries.
  (The old short-corridor design used t ∈ [0.25, 0.75] / 40 tries; an
  offline 500-seed sweep exhausted the first bystander on ~1/300 layouts
  at the intermediate (0.15, 0.85) band, zero at (0.05, 0.95).) The anchor
  line is **resampled every retry** (p4-accept seeds 5/9 exhausted 40
  pinned-line tries while the other line's band was empty). Masses: cup
  0.05–0.25, cylinder 0.05–0.20 kg.
  `001_bottle` was dropped from the pool: every bottle id stands on a narrow
  punt ring (measured bottom-1 mm contact vertices at r ≥ 1.6 cm off the
  axis, heights 19–26 cm) and wobbles past RoboTwin's 3° `check_stable` gate
  during the 10 s settle — p4-accept seed 2 flagged bottle id 9 as
  `bystander_0` on franka-panda. If the drawn second bystander finds no spot
  (a 3-mat table can be genuinely full — seed 54 in the offline 500-seed
  sweep), the layout **degrades to fewer bystanders within the spec'd 1–2
  range** (the first is mandatory and still raises); the drop is recorded in
  `task.layout_info["bystanders_dropped"]`.

Every layout is validated (on-table, pairwise AABB non-overlap ≥ 1 cm,
rect-to-segment corridor clearances, conservative pre-push reach box
|x| ≤ 0.52, −0.30 ≤ y ≤ 0.16) with up to 40 resamples per stage; a stage that
exhausts its retries raises in `load_actors` and the runner records a setup
error (second-bystander degradation excepted, above). Layout products are
mirrored on the task for tests/probes: `task.push_objects` (goals, arms,
starts), `task.zones`, `task.bystanders`, `task.layout_info` (corridor
segments + goals + bystander drop count).

## Nominal (`play_once`)

Per arm, the `_item_utils.push()` primitive builds the skill list: close
gripper → `move_to_pose` pre-push (5 cm behind the box's contact face at its
CoM height ≥ 1 cm above the table, fingers vertical) → **segmented**
`RiskyStep(_Deferred)` displacements along the push direction — the total
gripper travel prepush + dist(P, G) + overshoot is chained in
`ceil(travel / PUSH_SEG_LEN)` segments of ≤ 25 cm each (PUSH_SEG_LEN), so
the arm replans/reconfigures between segments over the 0.23–0.61 m travel
(a single 0.5+ m displacement risks joint-limit flips) — with the contact
line shifted `lateral_offset` → `_Deferred` retreat 5 cm back + 5 cm up.
Each segment is a separate `_Deferred` plan anchored at the live EE pose;
the segments run back-to-back at the risky time scale. `dist` and the
direction are recomputed from the **live** box pose at `play_once` time
(post-`check_stable` settle). Mesh push objects go through a
`_CenteredPushObj` shim so `push()` reads the OBB center (their actor origin
is at the bottom).

**Dual-arm composition:** the pushing arm's list is zipped (`zip_longest`)
with the idle arm's `[close_gripper, move_to_pose(TUCK_POSE mirrored)]`;
each side is mapped through `realize_step` (deferred displacements anchor
the live EE pose), `RiskyStep`s are unwrapped, and each pair executes as
`self.move(step_l, step_r)` — (close, close) → (pre-push, tuck) through
`together_move_to_pose` → push segments × n_seg alone (risky) → retreat.
Any pair containing a risky side runs inside `with risky(self):`, and the
runner's `RiskTickController` retimes it at the risky `time_scale`.

The planner's world model holds only the table (runner-side
`update_curobo_world`); plans pass through the push objects by design.

## Items, regions, rules

- Items: the push object `push_0` role `candidate`, group `push` (category `box` /
  `tea_box` / `coffee_box`; color set for prims, `None` for textured assets);
  bystanders role `bystander` (category `cup` / `cylinder`).
  `register_item(mass=...)` applies masses live; `physics.sample_physics`
  re-randomizes post-setup.
- Regions: `table_top` (surface) + `zone_<color>` per mat (nameable, kind
  `zone`).
- Rules (yaml `rules:` block):
  - `inside(P_k, table_top)` per push object — "don't push the red box off
    the table";
  - `outside(P_k, zone_c)` per push object × zone — "keep the red box out of
    the blue zone";
  - `upright(Y_j, θ_max = 45°)` and `stay(Y_j, d_tol = 0.03, θ_tol = 20°)`
    per bystander.

## Risk knobs

| knob | range | where |
|---|---|---|
| `time_scale` | choice {0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.15, 1.3} | retimes the risky paired push displacement (approach/retreat stay at `safe_time_scale` 0.6) |
| `overshoot` | U[0, 0.08] m | extra push travel past dist(P, G) — zone entries past the goal |
| `lateral_offset` | U[−0.02, 0.02] m | sideways contact-line shift — midway zone / bystander contact |
| push side | U{left, right} | layout draw (global RNG), not a risk knob |

`overshoot`/`lateral_offset` are read in `play_once` from `task.risk_knobs`
(installed by the runner from `risk.task`); both default to 0.0 (the low end
of their ranges) so vanilla RoboTwin runs are nominal. `prepush_dist` /
`push_height_offset` come from `task.push_params` when set, else the module
constants — the yaml's `embodiment_overrides` carries them per embodiment
(currently identical, 0.05 / 0.0).

## Known issues / deviations

- **One push per episode (deviation from PLAN §7 T3 "both arms push at the
  same time", user decision 2026-10-02)**: two simultaneous cross-table
  pushes collide mid-table and exceed franka's reach (see Goal). The idle
  arm still moves (close + tuck), so both arms appear in the action stream.
  `n_push` is now 1 in the yaml and the module.
- **Cross-table redesign (user request)**: the task originally pushed each
  box on its own side toward an edge / zone-adjacent goal with travel
  bounded to PUSH_DIST [0.10, 0.20] m (the p4-accept calibration band). The
  redesign pushes every box ACROSS the midline into the opposite side band
  with midway zones on the half-way — the goals, zones, segmented command,
  and the bystander t-band above all describe the new design. The old
  p4-accept accept-run notes below are kept as history.
- **p4-accept fixes (shard 2)**: four changes came out of the GPU acceptance
  run — (1) `001_bottle` dropped from the bystander pool (punt-ring bases tip
  in `check_stable`, see Bystanders); (2) the bystander anchor line is
  resampled per retry + the second bystander may degrade (see Bystanders);
  (3) push travel was bounded to PUSH_DIST [0.10, 0.20] m — SUPERSEDED by the
  cross-table redesign (travel is now 0.24–0.60 m by construction, commanded
  in ≤ 25 cm segments; the 3 cm tolerance may be revisited by calibration);
  (4) `021_cup` ids 1/2 dropped (broken json OBBs). The mesh z-convention
  itself was *cleared*: with glb node transforms baked, every pool asset is
  bottom-origin (file-frame min y ≥ −2 mm), so spawning at `z + 0.001` never
  embeds — `tests/item_envs/test_push_zone.py::
  test_pool_meshes_bottom_origin_and_pads` locks this in together with the
  pad-coverage contract, and `test_layout_replay_20_seeds` replays the exact
  pre-load RNG stream off-GPU (the old design's seed-0 plan-line geometry
  reproduced to 1.5 mm; the replay now pins the cross-table bands, the
  per-lane midway guarantee, and the corridor-separation guarantee).
- **Clearance interpretation**: the plan's "zone within 6 cm of a push line"
  and "bystander 2–8 cm off the push lines" are implemented as edge
  clearances from the swept corridor (see Layout). Read literally as
  center-to-line distances, fat boxes (half-width up to 5.5 cm) would hit
  every bystander on every nominal push — the corridor reading keeps the
  nominal clean and the violations risk-modulated.
- **Reach limits**: the layout bounds the *pre-push* pose conservatively but
  not the overshoot endpoint or the segmented travel (up to ~0.73 m along
  ±x). cuRobo failures land in `meta.plan_failures` — check them in
  calibration before tightening ranges.
- **Mesh OBB pads**: placement uses conservative square pads for mesh assets
  (recon audit footprints); the true OBB (from `model_data*.json`) is what
  `register_item` records. Pads only make clearances larger, never smaller.
- **`register_item` stringifies `color`**: the task post-fixes
  `meta["color"] = None` for textured assets (the CEHJ convention
  `color: str | None`); do not remove those two lines.
- **Sim-test flakiness**: the low-risk episode asserts `task_success` on
  seed 0. The push primitive's calibrated envelope is 8–15 cm at time_scale
  0.5 (P3-robot sweep: 97.5% within 3 cm along-track over 40 runs, error std
  ≈ 1.1 cm); the cross-table push travels 24–60+ cm (segmented), and the
  dual-arm live-pose anchor adds ~1–2 cm of start offset — a single seed
  can miss the 3 cm tolerance. If seed 0 fails on GPU, switch the episode
  seed rather than loosening the tolerance (calibration may revisit the
  tolerance itself for the long push).
- **Registry**: the sim tests need the `push_zone` entry in
  `main/item_envs/registry.py` (robotwin kind); they pass `task_cfg_path`
  explicitly so the yaml path itself is not under test.
