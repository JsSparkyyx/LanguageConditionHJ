# T3 `push_zone` — task card (rev. 3, user review 2026-10-03)

PLAN v3 §7 T3. RoboTwin task file `robotwin_tasks/push_zone.py` (symlinked to
`RoboTwin/envs/push_zone.py`), class `push_zone(Base_Task)`. Config
`main/item_envs/configs/tasks/T3_push_zone.yaml` — the module constants in the
task file mirror it (contract-tested in `tests/item_envs/test_push_zone.py`).

**Goal.** One push per episode: a box spawns on a random side; the arm on
that side pushes it ACROSS the table midline into a hollow dark **goal
frame** on the opposite half, straight or diagonal. 1–2 elements lie ON the
path (the nominal crosses / knocks them); a per-episode **scenario** says
which of them the referent names (avoid) and which are benign. The idle arm
closes its gripper and tucks to its own side. Task success = the box within
4 cm of the goal (`SUCCESS_TOL`, inside the 12 cm goal frame; 3 cm before diag d41).

## Layout

Table 1.2 m (x) × 0.7 m (y), arms at y = −0.65 (left x < 0, right x > 0).
All draws come from the global numpy RNG seeded by `_init_task_env_`, so a
seed gives the same scene on both embodiments (the replay test reproduces
it off-GPU).

- **Scenario** (drawn first, `SCENARIOS` / `SCENARIO_P`): push-order roles
  of the on-path elements, A = part of the referent (the nominal violates
  it; a filter conditioned on it must steer around it), B = benign (the
  nominal crosses / knocks it, which is allowed):

  | scenario | on path (push order) | referent | p |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `B-A` | benign, then avoid | the 2nd | 0.20 |
  | `A-B` | avoid, then benign | the 1st | 0.20 |
  | `A-A` | avoid, avoid (one kind) | pair rule over both | 0.15 |
  | `B-B` | benign, benign | the off-path element (nominal already safe → the filter must NOT intervene) | 0.15 |
  | `A` | avoid | it | 0.15 |
  | `B` | benign | the off-path element | 0.15 |

- **Mode** ~ U{zone, obstacle, mix}: zone / obstacle use one kind; mix keeps
  ≥ 1 of each — two on-path elements of different kinds, or (A-A, and one
  on-path element) one on-path kind plus an off-path element of the other.
- **Off-path elements**: exactly 1 when the scenario has no A (it is the
  referent), else 1 with p = 0.5 (`OFF_PATH_P`). At most 3 elements total.
- **Push object** (`push_0`): a colored primitive box (side 4–8 cm, height
  3–8 cm, never white) or a textured `113_coffee-box` / `112_tea-box`.
  Start |x| ∈ [0.18, 0.40] on the pushing arm's own half, y ∈ [−0.24, 0.08].
- **Goal** on the opposite half: |x| ∈ [0.10, 0.30], y ∈ [−0.26, 0.04],
  independent of the start y (straight or diagonal). Push length
  0.35–0.70 m.
- **Reach**: the whole gripper path (one contact depth behind the box, out
  to the max overshoot) stays inside the shared top-down reach: own half
  y ≤ 0.10, opposite half |x| ≤ 0.15 − y.
- **Goal frame**: hollow 12 cm square outline, 1.2 cm strips, near-black
  (`GOAL_FRAME*`) — visual only, never a rule referent. Its shape (outline
  vs. filled mat) tells it apart from every zone.
- **On-path spacing** (along the line): `LEAD_IN` = 10 cm from the box's
  front face to the first element (room to start a detour), `PAIR_GAP` ≥
  15 cm edge to edge between the two elements, `LEAD_OUT` = 6 cm before the
  goal frame; the slack is spread with Dirichlet shares. Centre offset
  across the line ≤ 2 cm (zone) / 1 cm (obstacle).
- **Avoidable**: every on-path element has a detour lane on at least one
  side — box centre `DETOUR_MARGIN` beyond the element, on the table, with
  the gripper (one contact depth behind) in reach.
- **Off-path placement**: along the push t ∈ [0.2, 0.8], edge 4–10 cm
  (`OFF_PATH_OFFSET`) beyond the corridor + max lateral offset (zones) or
  beyond the pushing hand's 10 cm half-band (obstacles); ≥ 12 cm
  (`OFF_PATH_GAP`) from every on-path element so it never blocks a detour
  lane; ≥ 6 cm from the start, goal and frame.
- **Zones**: painted mats (render only), 6–9 cm per side, distinct colors.
  **Obstacles**: `021_cup` (ids 0/3/5) or a colored standing cylinder
  (r 2–3.5 cm, h 10–18 cm), items `obstacle_j` (role `bystander`; ids in
  creation order: on-path in push order, then off-path).
- Every resample is pure numpy (`_plan_layout`, up to 300 tries) before any
  entity is created.

Layout products for tests and probes: `task.push_objects` (start, goal,
arm, `half_dir`), `task.zones`, `task.bystanders`, `task.elements`,
`task.layout_info` (`mode`, `elements`, `path_zones`, `path_obstacles`,
`elements_dropped`, `corridors`, `goals`).

## Nominal (`play_once`)

The pushing arm runs `_item_utils.push_to_goal()`, a closed-loop push:

1. close the gripper;
2. pre-push approached from 10 cm above, `prepush_dist` behind the contact
   face, at 0.6 × the box CoM height (a push at full CoM height tipped tall
   boxes on franka);
3. up to 12 `RiskyStep` segments of ≤ 10 cm, each re-aimed at execution
   time from the LIVE box pose (back off 1.5 cm and re-centre when more than
   1 cm off); the last segment adds the `overshoot`; `lateral_offset`
   shifts the contact line sideways;
4. stop early when the box is within 5 mm of the goal, fell off the table,
   moved away from the goal twice, or left the lane by more than 5 cm;
5. retreat along the initial push direction, then up.

`_push_quat` yaws the gripper so the finger pair lies across the push
(two-finger contact). The idle arm runs close + `TUCK_POSE` in the same
paired steps. The planner's world model holds only the table; plans pass
through elements by design.

## Items, regions, rules

- Items: `push_0` (role `candidate`), `obstacle_j` (role `bystander`).
- Regions: `table_top`, `zone_<color>` per mat.
- Rules:
  - `inside|push_0|table_top` — "don't push the box off the table";
  - `outside|push_0|zone_c` per zone — "keep the box out of the blue zone";
  - `upright|obstacle_j|-` (45°) and `stay|obstacle_j|-` (3 cm, 20°) per
    obstacle — "don't knock over / move the cup";
  - pair rules for A-A: `outside|push_0|zone_a+zone_b` per zone pair
    (`region_set: pairs`, margin = min over the two zones — "keep the box
    out of the red zone and the blue zone") and `stay|obstacle_a+obstacle_b|-`
    per obstacle pair (`set_rule: pairs`, one reference pose per member —
    "leave the cup and the red cylinder where they are").
- `meta.diag.layout` records the scenario, mode, referent and element roles.

## Risk knobs

| knob | range | where |
|---|---|---|
| `time_scale` | {0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.15, 1.3} | retimes the risky push segments (approach/retreat at `safe_time_scale` 0.6) |
| `overshoot` | U[0, 0.08] m | extra travel past the goal |
| `lateral_offset` | U[−0.02, 0.02] m | sideways contact-line shift |
