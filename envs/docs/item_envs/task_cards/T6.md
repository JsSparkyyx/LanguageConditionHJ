# T6 clean_pour — keep the beads out of the NAMED bowl (redesigned 2026-10-23)

Two designs share the item key `clean_pour` (the registry entry,
`T6_clean_pour.yaml`, the episode records and the `CANON_TASKS` index):

- **2026-10-23 design (current, Yuxuan)**: three bowls,
  `robotwin_tasks/bowl_pour.py`. Described first.
- **2026-10-04 design**: 2-3 bins and plates around a deep bowl,
  `robotwin_tasks/clean_pour.py`, left untouched for the runs collected
  with it. The second half of this card is its record, as it was.

## 2026-10-23 design (current): three bowls

### Why

T2 (`granular_pour`) has one rule, "spill no bead", so its sentence never
changes what the filter must do: T2 is not text-conditioned. On
2026-10-23 (Yuxuan) T2 left the training and evaluation tasks
(`main/lang/config.py` `TRAIN_TASKS`: T1, T3, T5, T6, T7), and T6 was
rebuilt on T2's pour. Three bowls that differ only in colour stand
together; the pour is meant for one of them (the target), and the
sentence names one of the other two:

- named = the bowl the careless pour is aimed at (the "hit" bowl, p 0.5):
  the nominal drops beads into it; the filter must pull the aim back over
  the target;
- named = the other bowl (p 0.5, **benign**): the nominal's beads land in
  an UNNAMED bowl, which is allowed; the filter must NOT step in.

### Scene (`robotwin_tasks/bowl_pour.py`, `class bowl_pour(granular_pour)`; yaml section `bowls`)

- T2's pour, unchanged: the handled source mug full of beads (6 mm, red),
  the same handle grasp, wrist roll and aim corrections.
- Three STATIC bowls: `002_bowl` base 5 (white), base 3 (blue) and base 7
  (green). The colour is each model's own texture. The colours are
  shuffled every episode:
  - the **target** bowl stands at T2's target spot (x = ±0.05 ± 0.03 m on
    the mug's side, y -0.15 ... -0.10 m; redrawn or shifted, as in T2,
    when the hand's footprint at the handle grasp would come too close),
    so the target's colour is random;
  - the two **other** bowls stand at the old T6 neighbour spots around the
    target, in random order: `far` (+y, behind the target as the robot
    sees it) and `across` (away from the mug), 90 deg apart, each turned
    by up to ±10 deg around the target. Each is kept 3 cm clear of the mug
    (counting 5 cm extra for its handle), 1.5 cm from the other bowl, 2 cm
    from the hand's footprint at the handle grasp, and on the table. If
    one does not fit, the scene build fails (`UnStableError`).
- Rim-to-rim gap between each other bowl and the target: U[9, 12] cm
  (`gap`, calibration below).
- The target is a plain `002_bowl`, not T2's deeper `002_bowl_deep`. Beads
  that land inside its opening stay in it (bead tracking below), so the
  plain bowl is deep enough; what matters is where the stream lands.

### Nominal (careless)

T2's pour with the aim point moved `aim_error` metres (U[0.16, 0.22])
toward the hit bowl (`bowl_pour._aim_bias_xy`; zero in T2). Which bowl is hit: on ur5-wsg
either of the two (50/50); on franka-panda always the `far` one
(`AIM_SLOTS`: in the old T6, franka's pour aimed `across` landed on the
far side, t6h below). A small error still lands in the target; a large
one splashes off the target's rim or pours into the hit bowl. T2's
`pour_offset` stays 0 (the aim error replaces it).

### Rules and referent

- `particles_outside|beads|bowl_<colour>` (consequence `exclude_particles`)
  per non-target bowl (`region_set: each`): the only rules a sentence
  names (Yuxuan, 2026-10-23). Training sentences: "keep the beads out of
  the blue bowl", "don't spill any beads into the white bowl", "don't drop
  any beads into the green bowl", "don't let any beads land in the blue
  bowl"; held-out template: "no beads in the white bowl"; held-out words:
  "container" for the bowl and "little balls" for the beads ("don't spill
  any little balls into the white container"). Every phrase says the
  colour (all three are bowls).
- The rule's column (region `bowl_<colour>`, kind `container`, role
  `keep_clean`): the bowl's footprint (outer wall + 2 mm), CAPPED
  `z_cap` = 1.2 cm above its rim plane (a bead flying over the bowl is not
  in it, one that drops in is) and FLOORED half a bead (`floor_bead_frac`
  0.5 x 6 mm = 3 mm) above its inside floor (`Region.z_floor`, review
  2026-10-23: the footprint reaches 2 mm past the outer wall, so without
  the floor a bead lying on the table against the outside of the blue or
  green bowl read "in the bowl").
- h: safe side = the closest bead's distance from entering the column /
  5 cm, clipped to [0, 1]; violated: by bead count, -0.5 for one bead, -1
  at 20 or more (`margins.particle_h`).
- `particles_inside|beads|bowl_src_interior+target_interior`, the spill
  rule (no bead outside the mug and the target): recorded, never named (no
  sentences from 2026-10-23). A bead in a non-target bowl breaks it too.
- Primary = the referent's `particles_outside`: the hit bowl or the other
  one, 50/50 (`referent`; diag override `ITEM_T6_REFERENT=hit|other`).
- In the way (`main/lang/scene_roles.py`): the hit bowl
  (`episode_info["hit"]`) and the bead set. The other bowl is benign;
  this design has no diagonal spot.

### Safe mode (the expert for safe demonstrations)

`ItemEpisodeRunner(..., nominal_mode="safe")` pins the yaml's
`safe_mode.risk`: the pour aimed at the target's centre (`aim_error` 0),
low (`pour_height` 0.05 m; the clamp from below still applies, as in T2)
and slow (`tilt_rate` 1 rad/s, `time_scale` 0.6). Keep rule for safe
demos (Yuxuan, 2026-10-23): no bead in the named bowl. In the final
check below (24 runs, base 5) it finished the job in 21 and put beads in
the named bowl in 1.

### Success

>= 50 % of the beads in the target bowl (`success_min_frac`; T2: 80 %).
Beads in the named bowl are a rule, not a success criterion.

### Words

Region ids `bowl_white`, `bowl_blue`, `bowl_green` (`objects.yaml`
region `bowl`: noun "bowl", held-out word "container"). Blue and green are
training colours; white is a `colors.fixed` word (a textured model's
colour: named, but never drawn for a primitive). The bowls are never
held-out models or colours, so T6 has no `heldout_item` rules.

## 2026-10-04 design (kept for the runs collected with it)

`robotwin_tasks/clean_pour.py`: 2-3 bins and plates around a deep target
bowl. The file is left untouched for the runs collected with it (RoboTwin
loads task files from one shared folder, so a running job would otherwise
pick up the new design mid-run). Its yaml values (neighbours, bins,
plates, referent 0.7 / 0.3) were in `T6_clean_pour.yaml` until
2026-10-23 (git history; the file now holds the new design). Everything
below is its record, as it was.

### Why this task exists

The user asked (2026-10-04, overnight) for extra tasks with **meaningful text
conditioning** and **object-oriented safety** (a consequence on an object,
not the robot's distance to it). T2 pours with ONE rule ("spill no bead"),
so its text never changes what the filter must do. T6 puts 2-3 open
containers around the pour target and names ONE of them: "don't get any
rice in the green bin", "keep the beads off the blue plate". The nominal's
aim is off toward one container (the "hit" one); whether the filter must
intervene — and which way it must pull the pour — depends on which
container the sentence names:

- referent = the hit container (p 0.7): the nominal contaminates it; the
  filter must pull the aim back toward the target (away from it);
- referent = another container (p 0.3, **benign**): the container angularly
  farthest from the aimed-at one (90 deg); the nominal splashes an UNNAMED
  container — allowed; the filter must NOT intervene.

### Scene (`robotwin_tasks/clean_pour.py`, `T6_clean_pour.yaml`)

T2's scene (`granular_pour`, unchanged): the handled source mug full of
beads on one side, the deep static target bowl near the centre, the same
handle grasp, wrist roll and aim corrections. Added:

- **Neighbours**: 2-3 STATIC primitive containers around the target — open
  square **bins** (side 8-10 cm, walls 3.5-5 cm, p 0.6) and flat square
  **plates** (side 10-12 cm, 1.2 cm thick, p 0.4), colors from {green,
  blue, yellow} (beads are red; white vanishes on the table). Slots at
  azimuth `far` (+y), `across` (away from the source, toward the table
  centre) and `far_across` (between them, only as a third — 45 deg from
  either, a splash toward one reached it: 3/4 benign violations involved it,
  t6c-g), jittered by 10 deg; the aim error points at an OUTER slot; the edge gap
  to the target's outer rim is 5-8 cm (an aim-0 pour splashes 1-10 cm past
  the rim: at a 1.5-3 cm gap 7/8 aim-0 pours contaminated one, t6b); kept
  3 cm clear of the source mug + handle and 2 cm clear of the hand's
  footprint at the handle grasp. A slot that does not fit is dropped
  (recorded in `meta.diag.task.slots_dropped`).
- **Regions**: one per neighbour, id `<kind>_<color>` (e.g. `bin_green`),
  role `keep_clean`, a square column over the footprint (+2 mm) CAPPED
  `z_cap` = 1.2 cm above its rim plane (`Region.z_cap`): a bead flying over
  a bin is not in it, one that drops in — or rests on a plate — is.

### Nominal

T2's pour with the aim point moved `aim_error` metres toward the hit
neighbour (`granular_pour._aim_bias_xy`, zero in T2). T2's `pour_offset`
is not used (0); `pour_height` 5-12 cm and `tilt_rate` 1-3 rad/s are
narrower than T2's (a high, fast pour scatters 3-6 cm, which swamped the
aim error).

### Rules and referent

- `particles_outside|beads|<kind>_<color>` per neighbour (`region_set:
  each`, T 1 mm) — "keep the rice out of the green bin", "don't spill any
  beads into the blue plate", "make sure no grains end up in the yellow
  box" (5 templates; a name another container answers to is dropped: with
  two bins every phrase says the color);
- `particles_inside|beads|bowl_src_interior+target_interior` (T2's
  spill-no-bead rule, secondary).

Primary = the referent's `particles_outside`.

### Risk knobs

| knob | range |
|---|---|
| `aim_error` | U[0.08, 0.16] m toward the hit neighbour (its near edge sits 13-17 cm from the target centre) |
| `pour_height` | U[0.05, 0.12] m above the target rim |
| `tilt_rate` | U[1.0, 3.0] rad/s |
| `time_scale` | {0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.15, 1.3} (transit) |

### Success

>= 50 % of the beads in the target (T2: 80 % — the aim error splashes some
by design). Contamination is a rule.

### Tuning history

  toward +y beyond the aim (T2's far-side bias) with 3-6 cm scatter: the far
  neighbour was contaminated at every aim, the benign scenario failed;
  franka hit the aim to < 1 cm but missed the mug handle 1/2 (below).
- franka handle misses (T2 too: 3/6 audit episodes): the -45 deg handle yaw
  points the handle diagonally AT the target; the fingers landed on the
  target's rim, 3-6 cm short of the handle. `granular_pour` now redraws /
  shifts the target until the hand's footprint behind the handle clears it
  (1.5 cm): t6b franka 8/8 grasps within 2.2 cm, all poured.
- t6b (aim 0, narrowed T2 knobs, 16 episodes): mean landing bias <= 2.7 cm
  both embodiments, in-target 42-100 %; splash past the rim p90 2-11 cm ->
