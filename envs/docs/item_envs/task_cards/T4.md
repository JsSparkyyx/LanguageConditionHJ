# T4 `granular_carry` — task card (user request 2026-10-03)

Carry a **brim-full** bowl of beads with **both arms** and place it on a grey
target pad without spilling a bead; keep spilled beads off the **named** mat.

- Registry: `granular_carry`, kind `robotwin` (`main/item_envs/registry.py`).
- Config: `main/item_envs/configs/tasks/T4_granular_carry.yaml`.
- Task file: `robotwin_tasks/granular_carry.py`
  (`class granular_carry(granular_pour)` — reuses the bead-skipping
  `check_stable`, `_rim_center`, `_beads_inside`), symlinked into
  `RoboTwin/envs/` by `install_robotwin_tasks.sh`.

## Layout

- **Bowl**: `002_bowl` id ~ U{1,2,3,5}, centre x ∈ [−0.04, 0.04],
  y ∈ [−0.02, 0.04] (between the arms), upright; mass U(0.10, 0.20) kg.
- **Beads** (6 mm, 0.5–2 g, red): the hex grid over-fills the bowl and every
  bead whose centre is more than 2 mm below the rim plane is kept —
  brim-full, ~115–235 beads depending on the bowl id. Top-layer beads within
  2.5 cm of the rim at the two pinch points are left out (the inner finger
  of a rim pinch otherwise plows them over the rim during the grasp).
- **Target pad**: painted (render-only) grey 16 cm square at
  start + (dx, −dy), dx ∈ [−0.05, 0.05], dy ∈ [0.14, 0.20] — the carry runs
  toward the robot (both arms reach that region on both embodiments).
- **Mats**: `zone_red` / `zone_blue`, one on each side of the carry path
  (side order drawn), centred at t ∈ [0.3, 0.7] of the start→pad segment,
  edge 2–5 cm clear of the carried bowl's outer wall.

## Nominal (`play_once`)

1. Gripper stiffness raised to 5000 (RoboTwin default 1000 — the loaded
   bowl slid and pivoted 13° in the pinches, diag job 22640284).
2. **Both arms pinch opposite rim points** together: right arm contact
   point 0 (+x rim), left arm contact point 2 (−x rim). A single rim pinch
   lets the bowl hang 16–23° tilted under gravity (measured in T2), which
   empties a brim-full bowl; two opposite pinches hang it level.
3. Lift by `carry_height`, carry over the pad, lower to 4 mm above the
   table — every motion is a chain of **paired lockstep steps** of 3 cm:
   both arms' plans are local screws (`task.plan_local`, min-jerk
   resampled) retimed to the SAME duration (`task.plan_duration_target`),
   so the two pinches move together. Carry speed 0.10 m/s × time_scale.
4. Open both grippers together, lift both 6 cm.

## Rules

- `particles_inside|beads|bowl_src_interior` — "don't spill the beans":
  per-bead 2-D distance inside the bowl's footprint column, soft-min over
  beads (T = 1 mm). One spilled bead makes it negative. **Primary.**
- `particles_outside|beads|zone_red` / `…|zone_blue` — "keep the beans off
  the red mat": one rule per mat, so the referent picks the side.

## Risk knobs

| knob | range | effect |
|---|---|---|
| `time_scale` | {0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.15, 1.3} | carry speed (lift + carry); lowering uses `safe_time_scale` |
| `carry_tilt_deg` | U[−6, 6] | bowl tilt held during the carry: a differential arm height set up in the lift and removed before the lowering; the sign picks the side the top layer spills toward |
| `carry_height` | U[0.04, 0.12] m | lift height |

## Success

The bowl rests on the pad: centre within 3 cm of the pad centre, upright
within 10°. Spill is a rule, not a success criterion (PLAN §7).

## Known issues

- Brim-full means the spill rule is very sensitive: a single bead bouncing
  out on the grasp or the release makes the episode a violation even at the
  lowest risk; calibration reports the base rate per cell.
- The two pinches are independent PD-driven arms; lockstep steps keep their
  relative error small, but long fast carries can still squeeze / stretch
  the bowl slightly.
