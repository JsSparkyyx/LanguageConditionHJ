# T4 `granular_carry` — task card (user request 2026-10-03, rev. 2)

Pick up a **brim-full** bowl of beads with **one arm** (the arm on the bowl's
side), carry it to a grey target pad and place it there without spilling a
single bead.

- Registry: `granular_carry`, kind `robotwin` (`main/item_envs/registry.py`).
- Config: `main/item_envs/configs/tasks/T4_granular_carry.yaml`.
- Task file: `robotwin_tasks/granular_carry.py`
  (`class granular_carry(granular_pour)` — reuses the bead-skipping
  `check_stable`, `_rim_center`, `_beads_inside`), symlinked into
  `RoboTwin/envs/` by `install_robotwin_tasks.sh`.

## Revision history

- rev. 1 (bimanual): both arms pinched opposite rim points and moved in
  paired lockstep steps; a forbidden mat lay on each side of the path.
- rev. 2 (user review 2026-10-03): **one arm** — left arm when the bowl is on
  the left half, right arm otherwise — and **no mats**: the only constraint
  is "spill no bead".

## Layout

- **Side** ~ U{left, right}; `carry_arm` = the arm on that side.
- **Bowl**: `002_bowl` id ~ U{1,2,3,5}, centre |x| ∈ [0.15, 0.25] on that
  side (`START_ABS_X`), y ∈ [−0.06, 0.04] (`START_Y`), upright; mass
  U(0.10, 0.20) kg (`CARRY_BOWL_MASS_RANGE`).
- **Beads** (radius 6 mm, 0.5–2 g, red): a hex grid (layer pitch 12.6 mm)
  over-fills the bowl and every bead whose centre lies at least
  `BRIM_CLEAR` = 10 mm below the rim plane is kept (at most `N_BEADS_MAX` =
  240). The layer pitch quantizes the fill: the top layer sits 10.9 / 15.1 /
  12.8 / 19.3 mm below the rim for bowl ids 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 (117 / 117 / 177 /
  172 grid beads). (12 mm left id 1 a full layer lower — 74 beads, 23.5 mm.)
  Top-layer beads near the pinch (`PINCH_CLEAR_R` 5 cm, `PINCH_CLEAR_DEPTH`
  2.5 cm) are left out of the grid, but the settle largely refills that
  void — the shallow pinch below is what actually stops grasp spills.
- **Target pad**: painted (render-only) grey 16 cm square at
  start + (dx, −dy), dx ∈ [−0.06, 0.06], dy ∈ [0.12, 0.18] (toward the
  robot), with |x| ≥ 0.10 so the pad stays on the carrying arm's half.
  The pad is a visual goal marker, never a rule referent.

## Nominal (`play_once`)

All motions are chains of **absolute-target steps** (`_steps_to`): position
and quaternion interpolated in ≤ `CARRY_STEP` = 3 cm pieces, each planned as
a local screw (`task.plan_local`, min-jerk resampled) and retimed to
`len / speed` (`task.plan_duration_target`). Absolute targets stop lag from
compounding the way the rev. 1 live-relative steps did.

1. Raise gripper stiffness to 5000 (RoboTwin default 1000 — the loaded bowl
   slid and pivoted in the pinch).
2. Grasp the outer rim at the contact point facing the arm
   (`GRASP_CONTACT_IDS`), **shallow and wall-centred**: the grasp target
   stops `GRASP_DIS` short of the contact point along the approach (module default 0.02 m;
   `embodiment_overrides`: ur5 0.0225, franka 0.015) and moves `GRASP_RADIAL_OUT` =
   6 mm radially outward. The default deep pinch put the inner finger into
   the top layer and shoved the bowl ~1 cm outward while closing, flicking
   2–7 beads out (bead speeds 0.3–0.9 m/s) before the lift. Sweep (diag
   d15–d17, low risk): grasp_dis 0.02 → franka 0 beads, ur5 1 bead per
   episode; 0.025 → 0 beads but ur5 slipped 1/3; ≥ 0.03 → the grip fails.
3. Lift off 1.5 cm (`LIFTOFF`), then **level**: rotate about the TCP
   (EE + 12 cm along EE x) until the bowl's up axis is vertical, up to 2
   iterations, 1° tolerance. A single rim pinch otherwise lets the bowl hang
   tilted under gravity, which empties a brim-full bowl.
4. Lift to `carry_height`, tilting by `carry_tilt_deg` about the axis across
   the carry direction (risky).
5. Carry over the pad at `CARRY_SPEED` = 0.10 m/s × time_scale (risky).
6. Re-level, lower in a closed loop (≤ 3 steps) to 4 mm above the table.
7. Open the gripper, retreat 6 cm up. The idle arm stays put.

## Rules

- `particles_inside|beads|bowl_src_interior` — "don't spill the beans":
  per-bead signed 2-D distance inside the bowl's footprint column (outer
  radius + 2 mm), soft-min over beads (T = 1 mm). One spilled bead makes it
  negative. The only rule, and the primary.

## Risk knobs

| knob | range | effect |
|---|---|---|
| `time_scale` | {0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.15, 1.3} | lift + carry speed; lowering uses `safe_time_scale` 0.6 |
| `carry_tilt_deg` | U[−25, 25] | bowl tilt held during the carry, about the axis across the carry direction; the sign picks the spill side |
| `carry_height` | U[0.04, 0.12] m | lift height |

## Success

The bowl rests on the pad: centre within 3 cm of the pad centre
(`SUCCESS_TOL`), upright within 10° (`SUCCESS_TILT_DEG`). Spill is a rule,
not a success criterion (PLAN §7).

## Measured (low risk = 0.5x, no tilt, 6 cm lift)

| setting | ur5-wsg | franka-panda |
|---|---|---|
| default deep pinch (diag 22640897) | 3/3 placed, 4-7 beads out at the grasp | 3/3 placed, 1 bead out near release |
| grasp_dis 0.0225 (ur5) / 0.015 (franka) — final (d17, d18, d20) | 9/10 placed, 8/10 spill-free (1 slip: heaviest bowl, 0.32 kg; 1 bead at release) | 6/6 placed, 5/6 spill-free (3 beads at 5.4 s) |
| grasp_dis 0.015 on ur5 (d20) | 40-50 beads flicked out at the grasp on bowl id 1 | — |
| gripper stiffness 20000 (d19) | 20-145 beads out: the stiff pinch squeezes and jerks the bowl | same |

Tilt (held, after leveling): reached — 19.2 deg max for a 20 deg command.
At 12 deg / 1.3x neither robot spilled (d18); at 20 deg franka still kept
every bead (the top layer sits 11-19 mm under the rim and the packed beads
do not flow) — hence the widened U[-25, 25] tilt range; calibration
measures the actual spill rate per cell.

The physics randomizer resamples the bowl mass (mass_scale x the category
nominal: 0.19-0.32 kg) after `CARRY_BOWL_MASS_RANGE` — the heaviest draws
are the ones that slip.

## Known issues

- Brim-full means the spill rule is very sensitive: one bead bouncing out
  on the grasp or the release makes the episode a violation even at the
  lowest risk. Calibration reports the base rate per cell.
- The held tilt peaks briefly at 6–10° during the lift-off before leveling
  converges; the median stays low.
