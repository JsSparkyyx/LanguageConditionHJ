Constraint Sentence Review

The sentences the filter is trained and tested with, after the 2026-10-20 rewrite: five plain sentences for each named rule, four for training and one held out for the out-of-distribution test. A obj / region / material slot is filled from the scene ("the red block", "the green mat", "beads").

  • 211 wordy templates replaced by 30 plain ones (6 rules x 5).
  • "Don't move" never says touch, bump or knock: in T1 the same object also has "don't touch".
  • "Don't spill" never says "keep the beads in": in T2 and T6 they are poured out on purpose.
  • Keep-on-the-table is no longer named (it is still measured, never used).
  • Plain slot words: mat, bin, plate, table; beads are "beads" or "balls" ("little balls" only in the held-out word test). Old recordings are re-named to these words when they are loaded.

Don't move

stay

T1 selective_avoid, T3 push_zone, T7 gap_place

The named object must not be moved more than 3 cm (T1), 1.5 cm (T3) or 2 cm (T7); tipping it counts as moving it. Touching it, or a small push that moves it less than the limit, is allowed. In T1 the same object always also has an avoid rule (don't touch).

Training (4)

  1. don't move obj
  2. leave obj where it is
  3. keep obj where it is
  4. make sure obj stays in place

OOD test (1)

  1. don't let obj move

Don't spill

contain_particles

T2 granular_pour, T4 granular_carry, T6 clean_pour (the spill rule)

No bead may land outside the containers. T4: the beads stay in the carried bowl. T2 and T6: the beads are POURED from the mug into the target bowl on purpose; a bead in either container is fine, a bead on the table is the violation.
